            <h2>What is mysearch.one?</h2>
            <p>mysearch.one is a versatile search tool that brings together multiple search engines and neural services in one place. Instead of being limited to one provider, you can easily choose the best option for your query—whether you need traditional search results or an AI-powered answer. This unified platform streamlines your search experience with a simple and intuitive interface.</p>
            <p><strong>mysearch.one is not a search engine, it doesn't search for anything, it doesn't use AI to provide answers.</strong> mysearch.one just redirects your queries to your chosen search engines or AI services.</p>
            <h2>How to use mysearch.one?</h2>
            <p>Just type your search query into the box. If you want to specify a particular search engine or service, add a bang command (a short keyword preceded by an exclamation mark (!), slash (/), dash (-), colon (:), or at sign (@)) at the end of your query.</p>

            <p>All bang commands work interchangeably — you no longer need to switch keyboard layouts or use inconvenient symbols. Feel free to use !, /, -, :, or @ — whichever is most convenient for you. For example, !google, /google, -google, :google, and @google all work exactly the same way.</p>

            <h2>Why use AI directly from the search bar?</h2>
            <p>Imagine you search for "how to cook oatmeal." With a traditional search engine, you might first have to read a long article about the history of oatmeal, learn about its benefits and drawbacks, and only then find a short note saying it needs to be boiled for 5–15 minutes. All that extra information can be overwhelming when you just want a simple answer.</p>
            <p>A neural network, on the other hand, gives you a precise and ready answer without all the extra words. It tells you exactly what you need to know right away, and if something isn’t clear, you can ask follow-up questions in a chat format. This makes the whole process much more efficient and user-friendly.</p>
            <p>That’s why mysearch.one lets you choose a neural network as your search engine. You can use ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, or OpenWebUI to get fast, clear answers tailored to your needs.</p>
